Coding, web & software documentation

5 Platforms

freeCodeCamp

A complete, interactive coding curriculum covering responsive web design, JavaScript, Python, SQL, and free project-based certifications.

freecodecamp.org

CS50 (Harvard University)

Harvard’s world-famous introduction to computer science, Python, SQL, cybersecurity, and AI — 100% free and self-paced.

cs50.harvard.edu

The Odin Project

A rigorous, open-source, project-driven path into full-stack web development using Git, HTML/CSS, JavaScript, React, and Node.js.

theodinproject.com

MDN Web Docs

The definitive official documentation and learning guides for HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and web APIs maintained by Mozilla.

developer.mozilla.org

Full Stack Open (Helsinki)

University of Helsinki’s free deep dive into modern web development with React, Redux, Node.js, TypeScript, GraphQL, and Docker.

fullstackopen.com

Data, AI, maths & core sciences

4 Platforms

Kaggle Learn

Hands-on, bite-sized free micro-courses on Python, Pandas, SQL, data visualization, and intro machine learning with real datasets.

kaggle.com/learn

Khan Academy

World-class free lessons in mathematics, linear algebra, statistics, physics, chemistry, biology, and economics from school to college level.

khanacademy.org

MIT OpenCourseWare

Complete lecture notes, problem sets, and video lectures from real MIT undergraduate and graduate courses in engineering, CS, and maths.

ocw.mit.edu

Learn Prompting

Open-source, research-backed curriculum teaching structured prompt engineering, LLM evaluation, and practical AI workflows for students.

learnprompting.org

5 Platforms

Figma Learn

Official free tutorials and documentation on wireframing, auto-layout, design systems, and interactive prototyping in Figma.

help.figma.com

Grow with Google / Skillshop

Free Google training in digital marketing, analytics, search engine optimization, and career readiness.

grow.google

Coursera & edX (Audit Mode)

Audit thousands of university courses for free from top global institutions by selecting “Audit the course” on individual modules.

coursera.org → · edx.org →

Canva Design School

Beginner-friendly visual design lessons covering typography, colour theory, branding, and social media layout fundamentals.

canva.com/design-school

HubSpot Academy

Free courses and certifications in SEO, content marketing, email copywriting, inbound sales, and social media strategy.

academy.hubspot.com

frontendmentor.io
