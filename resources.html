Hand-picked, genuinely free (or free-to-audit) platforms that we recommend to students starting out. Every link goes to the platform’s official site.

Always free? Some platforms let you audit courses for free but charge for a certificate. That’s fine — the learning is what matters. You do not need a certificate to build skills or a portfolio.
