About FuturePath

Why FuturePath exists and how we build it.

Honest Guidance

Free, practical advice grounded in real requirements. Always verify admissions and deadlines on official portals.

Clarity for every student, regardless of background

FuturePath helps students choose a degree, build skills and find opportunities. We aim to be honest: we label sample data, never promise income, and link to official sources wherever we can.

This site began as a simple idea — that deciding your future shouldn’t depend on who you know or how much you can pay for advice. So we keep everything free, plain-spoken and grounded in real information. Where we’re still building (universities, scholarships and internships listings), we say so clearly rather than inventing details.

What we believe

Honesty first

We label sample data, never promise income, and admit what we don’t know yet.

Official sources

For fees, deadlines and admissions, we point you to the real websites — not our memory.

Practical guidance

Every recommendation comes with a next step you can actually take today.
