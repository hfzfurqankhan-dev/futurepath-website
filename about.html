About FuturePath

Why FuturePath exists and how we build it.

Honest Guidance

Free, practical advice grounded in real requirements. Always verify admissions and deadlines on official portals.

Clarity for every student, regardless of background

FuturePath helps students across Pakistan choose a degree, explore modern career paths, build in-demand skills, and find verified academic opportunities. We follow a strict accuracy standard: we label illustrative examples clearly, never promise guaranteed employment or income, and link directly to official university, regulatory, and scholarship portals.

This platform began with a simple conviction — that deciding your future after Matric, Intermediate, or graduation shouldn’t depend on expensive private counselling or word-of-mouth rumours. Everything on FuturePath is 100% free, transparent, and built so you can verify every claim yourself.

100% Independent & FreeZero sponsored rankings, transparent scoring, and direct regulatory links.

What we believe

Honesty first

We label illustrative student scenarios, never promise guaranteed income, and never invent university fees or admission cutoffs.

Official sources

For fees, deadlines, accreditation, and admissions, we point you directly to official HEC, PMDC, PEC, PCP, and university websites.

Practical guidance

How FuturePath recommendations work

Unlike opaque career tests that hide how they pick your results, FuturePath uses an open, inspectable scoring model so you always understand why a career or degree was suggested:

Step 1 · Weighted Matching

Transparent 3-2-2-1-1 Scoring

In the Career Finder, each option you select in Steps 2–6 is compared against 14 career profiles: Core Interests (3 pts), Natural Strengths (2 pts), Preferred Work Activities (2 pts), Subjects Enjoyed (1 pt), and Work Environment (1 pt). Your score is calculated as Match % = round((Matched Weighted Points ÷ Total Weighted Points of Your Selected Options) × 100), displaying the top 3 matching careers with every matched tag shown openly.

Step 2 · Stage-Aware Guidance

Education-Level Tailoring

Whether you are in Matric/O-Levels, Intermediate/A-Levels, a Bachelor’s degree, or a Master’s programme, FuturePath adapts its next-step advice — from choosing your FSc/ICS/ICom stream and preparing for MDCAT/ECAT/NET/LAT to building a graduation portfolio.

Step 3 · Official Verification

Direct Regulatory & Portal Links

Every career links to related degrees in our 22-degree explorer (verified against HEC, NCEAC, PMDC, PEC, PCP, PNMC, PCATP & PBC), connecting to 18 universities, 12 scholarships, and 9 internship portals.

Frequently asked questions

Does FuturePath accept payments from universities or training institutes to rank them higher?

▾

No. FuturePath is 100% independent and has zero sponsored rankings. Universities, scholarships, and free learning platforms are listed solely on academic relevance, HEC/regulatory recognition, and usefulness for Pakistani students.

Where is my checklist and roadmap progress stored?

▾

All of your Career Finder selections, Degree Comparisons, Skills Roadmap checkboxes, and University/Scholarship/Internship checklists are saved privately in your browser’s localStorage on your own device. We do not track or sell your personal data.

How often are external links and regulatory details checked?

▾

All university, scholarship, internship, and learning resource links were verified against official portals in November 2026. Because admission dates, fee structures, and government schemes change each cycle, always confirm final deadlines directly on the linked official website.
