What to check before you apply

HEC & council recognition

Confirm the university and your specific degree are recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and the relevant statutory council: PEC (Engineering), NCEAC (Computing), PMDC (MBBS/BDS), PCP (Pharm-D), PNMC (Nursing), PCATP (Architecture), or PBC (Law).

Total 4-to-5 year cost

Look beyond first-semester tuition: calculate per-credit-hour fees, annual fee revisions, admission/security deposits, transport, hostel charges, and exam fees.

Deadlines & entry tests

Note the exact admission test required (MDCAT, ECAT, NUST NET, FAST NU, NTS NAT, HEC LAT, or SAT), test series dates, and merit aggregate formula.

Faculty & facilities

Inspect the department’s full-time PhD/industry faculty list, teaching hospitals or lab equipment, library access, and alumni placement record on the official site.

Public vs private universities in Pakistan

Public sector universities generally offer lower subsidized tuition fees, large campuses, and intense merit competition; private sector universities often feature smaller class sizes, modern lab infrastructure, and strict academic calendars, usually at a higher per-semester cost. Many top private universities (such as LUMS, AKU, FAST, and GIKI) also offer generous need-based financial aid and outreach scholarships. Neither sector is automatically “better” — what matters is accreditation, department strength, and affordability for your family.

Always verify on the official site. Fees, eligibility rules, and admission deadlines change every academic year. Confirm all details directly on the university’s official website and the HEC Recognised Universities portal before applying.
