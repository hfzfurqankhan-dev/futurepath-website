Find the right university

A practical checklist for choosing the right university

  • Is the programme HEC-recognised?
  • Total cost, not just tuition
  • Admission test, deadline and merit

Choosing a university is one of the biggest financial and time decisions you’ll make as a student. A verified listing tool is on our roadmap; until then, here’s a practical checklist you can use to evaluate any university yourself.

What to check before you apply

Recognition

For Pakistan, confirm the university and your specific programme are recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC).

Total cost

Look beyond tuition: admission fees, transport, hostel and living costs add up fast.

Deadlines & tests

Note the admission test (e.g. university entry tests), form deadline and merit formula.

Faculty & facilities

Check faculty qualifications, labs, library and industry links on the official site.

Public vs private

Public universities usually have lower fees and strong merit competition; private universities may offer smaller classes, modern facilities and flexible intake, often at higher cost. Neither is automatically “better” — what matters is fit with your goals, budget and the recognition of the specific programme.

Always verify on the official site. Fees, eligibility and deadlines change every year. Confirm everything on the university’s own website and the HEC website before you decide.
