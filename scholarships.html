Scholarships

Types of scholarships to look for and how to apply honestly.

Scholarships can cover part or all of your study costs, but competition is real and the honest ones are always free to apply for. A verified, dated listings tool is on our roadmap; until then, here’s how to find and apply for scholarships the right way.

Types of scholarships to look for

🏆Merit-based

Awarded for strong academic results. Many universities offer automatic merit aid for top marks.

🤝Need-based

For students who can’t afford full fees. You’ll usually show family income documents.

🏁Government

In Pakistan, the HEC runs and lists several scholarship and fee-support schemes.

🌍International

Well-known programmes include Fulbright (USA), DAAD (Germany), Chevening (UK) and Commonwealth scholarships.

How to apply

Keep a folder ready with your transcripts, CNIC or B-form, recent photos, recommendation letters and a short statement of purpose. Apply before the deadline on the official site, keep copies of everything, and track what you’ve submitted. Treat each application seriously — quality beats quantity.

Scholarship scams are common. No real scholarship asks you to pay a “processing fee” to receive money, share your bank PIN, or cash a cheque and send money back. If it sounds too easy, it is almost certainly a scam.
