Types of scholarships to look for

Merit & outreach aid

Awarded for top board/entry-test marks or outreach programmes (such as LUMS NOP and IBA NTHP) that fund talented students from underserved areas.

Need-based endowments

HEC Need-Based aid, PEEF, and Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal support students who have secured admission on merit and demonstrate verified household financial need.

Federal & provincial schemes

Federal (Benazir/Ehsaas Undergraduate, PM Youth) and provincial endowments (PEEF, BEEF, SEEF) fund thousands of public-university students annually.

International postgraduate

Fully funded global Master’s and PhD programmes include Fulbright (USA), Chevening (UK), DAAD (Germany), Commonwealth, and Erasmus Mundus (EU).

How to prepare a strong scholarship application

Keep a digital and physical folder ready with attested transcripts, CNIC/B-Form, domicile, family income verification, two academic recommendation letters, and a tailored Statement of Purpose (SOP). For international scholarships, start preparing 8–10 months before intake deadlines so you can complete IELTS/TOEFL or GRE tests and write multiple drafts of your personal essays.

Scholarship scams are common. No real scholarship asks you to pay a “processing fee,” “visa deposit,” or “agent charge” to receive funding. Apply only through official government, university, or embassy portals.
