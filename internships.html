Step-by-step internship application guidance

1. Time your applications

In Pakistan, major summer internship intakes (banks, FMCGs, telecoms, and large software houses) open between February and April for June–August cohorts. Global remote programmes like Google Summer of Code (GSoC) and Outreachy open applications between January and March.

2. Combine portals with direct outreach

Don’t rely only on job boards where 1,000+ students click “Easy Apply.” Shortlist 20 software houses, hospitals, law chambers, or design studios in your city, find a team lead or HR manager on LinkedIn, and send a polite note linking directly to your portfolio project.

3. Tailor every submission

Read the internship description carefully and mirror its core tools in your CV’s top project bullets (for example, highlighting React/JavaScript for a frontend role or Excel/SQL for a business analyst internship).

4. Prepare for technical & behavioural screening

Be ready to screen-share one project you built from scratch, explain a bug or design problem you solved, and ask 2 thoughtful questions about how interns are mentored on the team.

Zero-experience student guide: how to stand out in 3 weeks

Every professional started with zero experience. If your CV currently feels empty, do not wait for a company to give you permission to build skills — create your own proof of work in three focused weeks:

Week 1 · Pick 1 Problem

Solve a real local problem

Instead of copying a generic calculator tutorial, pick a small real-world problem: redesign a cluttered local clinic website in Figma, clean a public Pakistan dataset in Python/Excel, or draft a 5-article SEO content hub for a student society.

Week 2 · Document Publicly

Publish with a clear README

Upload your code to GitHub (with screenshots and setup instructions), publish your UI case study on Behance, or share your interactive dashboard on Google Looker Studio / Tableau Public so anyone can inspect it in one click.

Week 3 · Pitch with Proof

Send 15 project-first pitches

Use the Cold-Email / LinkedIn Pitch Builder above to message 15 companies. Leading with “I built this working prototype using your tech stack” puts you ahead of 90% of applicants who send a blank CV.

What to expect during an internship

Most student internships last 6–12 weeks during the summer or final semester. Many reputable companies pay a travel/meal stipend or junior salary, while some early-stage learning internships are unpaid. Focus on teams where a senior engineer, designer, clinician, or analyst reviews your work weekly.

Watch out for fake “pay-for-certificate” internships. Never pay a registration, portal activation, or certificate fee for an internship. If a company asks you to pay money to work for them, it is a scam — walk away immediately.
