Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Straightforward answers about how FuturePath works, how we verify academic information, and how to use our tools.

How does FuturePath calculate career and degree recommendations?

▾

FuturePath uses a 100% transparent point-scoring formula rather than a hidden “AI black box.” When you use the Career Finder, every option you check in Steps 2–6 is compared against our 14 career profiles using fixed weights: Core Interests = 3 pts each, Natural Strengths = 2 pts each, Preferred Work Activities = 2 pts each, Subjects Enjoyed = 1 pt each, and Preferred Work Environment = 1 pt each. For each career, your Match % = round((Matched Weighted Points ÷ Total Weighted Points of Your Selected Options) × 100). We display the top 3 matching careers along with stage-specific next steps based on your selected education level (Matric, Intermediate, Bachelor’s, or Master’s).

Why doesn’t FuturePath display exact university tuition fees or exact admission merit cutoffs?

▾

Pakistani universities update their per-credit-hour tuition fees, hostel charges, and admission merit cutoffs every single intake cycle. Websites that publish static fee tables quickly become outdated and mislead families. Instead of guessing or inventing numbers, we list verified structural facts (sector, city, accreditation councils, known programmes, and required entry tests such as MDCAT, ECAT, NET, NAT, or LAT) and link you straight to each university’s official portal alongside our 6-point cost evaluation checklist.

Can I start earning money online immediately as a beginner student?

▾

No legitimate online path guarantees immediate income. All timelines on FuturePath are illustrative estimates: real skill-based earning — whether freelance web development, data analysis, UI/UX design, video editing, or online tutoring — typically requires an estimated 3 to 6+ months of dedicated skill building and 3 to 5 completed portfolio projects before you win your first paying client. Anyone asking you to pay a registration fee, security deposit, or ID card fee for “guaranteed daily typing/clicking income” is running a scam.

Can Pre-Medical students in Pakistan switch into Computer Science, IT, or Data Science?

▾

Yes, at most universities. Under official HEC & NCEAC policy, FSc Pre-Medical students with at least 50% marks can take direct admission into BS Computer Science, BS Software Engineering, BS Artificial Intelligence, BS Data Science, BS IT, and BS Cybersecurity, and must pass two deficiency Mathematics courses totaling 6 credit hours within their first year (first two semesters). Important exception: some individual universities (such as FAST-NUCES) require Pre-Medical applicants to have passed or registered for board-level Additional Mathematics prior to admission. Always verify the exact rule on your target university’s admission portal.
