AI in your career

Three ways students can use AI right now

Used well, AI is a study partner, a creative collaborator and a career coach — not a replacement for thinking.

Learn faster

Summarise lectures, quiz yourself, and get instant explanations of tough topics in plain language.

Create and produce

Draft essays and code, brainstorm ideas, and polish your writing — then make it your own.

Prepare for careers

Practise interviews, refine your CV, and research roles and companies before you apply.

Use AI honestly. Don’t submit AI work as your own in exams or assignments, and never trust AI output blindly — always verify facts. AI is a tool to think with, not a substitute for thinking.
